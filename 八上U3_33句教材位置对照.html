三、Section B 1b 三帖全文（P26–P27，可直接对照翻书）

以下为教材逐字原文，加粗为本表已收录句。检查时可逐句比对。

Forum — Tom（5 hours ago，P26） Some people think that friends should be alike, but some other people think that friends should be different. What do you think? Should friends be similar or different? Forum — Matt（4 hours ago，P26） I think a good friend is like a mirror. You can understand people by knowing their friends. My friend, Stephen, is a great example.  ← 未收录 We often hang out together because we have similar hobbies and interests. For example, we both love sports. He plays badminton better than I do, so he always wins our matches. Because of that, I try harder when I practise, and now I'm getting better at badminton. Stephen and I are similar in other ways. He is as serious as me. When we aren't playing sports, we enjoy reading together. I read more history books than he does, and he reads a lot of novels. We often share interesting books with each other. Forum — Diana（3 hours ago，P27） I don't agree.  ← 未收录 For me, friends who are different make me better. I think we should respect each other's differences and learn from each other. For example, I am kind of shy, but my best friend, Yuan Lei, is outgoing. She has a great sense of humour too. Thanks to her, I am becoming more outgoing. Now I think meeting new people is really fun! I have another friend, Zhang Yong.  ← 未收录 He is not afraid to give his opinion, especially when he thinks I am making a mistake. I am less straightforward than he is, but I am glad to have an honest friend.

2. 5 句非逐字收录（已在上表标注「有删改」）

句子教材原样改动
It is more colourful than last year.I think it is more colourful than last year.删 I think（保留比较级主干）
Emma is funnier than me.And she is funnier than me.去 And，she → Emma（否则指代不清）
We're different in many ways, but together, we make a great team!You see, we're different in many ways, but together, we make a great team!删 You see（口语填充词）
There are more activities this year.There are more students and more activities this year.本句取 3a 第 1 句，第 7 句取 2a 原句，两句互补不重复
Emma always gets up earlier than me, but I work as hard as her!Emma always gets up earlier than me. Many people say I'm lazier than Emma, but that's not true! I work as hard as her!3a 第 5 句本身即此写法，与教材一致

3. 1 句已更换（重点核对）

#12 原为 Sam is as serious as Tom.，教材查无此句——3b 第 3 题里 Sam/Tom 搭配的是 smart（问句 Is Tom smarter than Sam? / 答句 I think Sam is as smart as Tom.），serious 只出现在 3b 第 2 题、比较对象是 your cousin。原句是把两道题的形容词与人名拼出来的。已换成 3b 第 3 题答句真原文。同时另补了 Matt 帖的 He is as serious as me.（#22），所以 serious 这个考点没有丢失。

本单元沿用英式拼写：colourful（#2）、practise（#20）、humour（#29），与人教版 2024 词汇表主词条一致（词汇表注明 humour (=humor)）。另外 than me / than her / as slim as me 等口语化代词是教材原文写法，不建议"纠正"成 than I。
