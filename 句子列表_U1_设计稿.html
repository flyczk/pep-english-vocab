Unit 1 Happy Holiday

PEP英语八上 · 单元总览
已掌握 0 / 18 句
学习中 0 · 未练 18

重点考点

12 句
  • 01

    Where did you go on holiday?

    你假期去了哪里？

  • 02

    I didn't go anywhere special, but I tried something new — I worked as a tour guide at a museum.

    我没去什么特别的地方，但尝试了新事物——我在博物馆当了一回导游。

  • 03

    Did you go anywhere interesting?

    你去什么有趣的地方了吗？

  • 04

    Did you do anything interesting on your holiday?

    你假期做了什么有趣的事吗？

  • 05

    No, nothing special. But I met someone interesting.

    没有，没什么特别的。不过我遇到了一个有趣的人。

  • 06

    Yes, I went to Yunnan.

    是的，我去了云南。

  • 07

    Did you go with anyone?

    你是和别人一起去的吗？

  • 08

    Yes, I went with my family.

    是的，我和家人一起去的。

  • 09

    Did you eat anything special?

    你吃了什么特别的东西吗？

  • 10

    Yes, we did! Everything tasted good, especially the steamed chicken soup. Everyone loved it.

    是的！每样东西都好吃，尤其是汽锅鸡。大家都很喜欢。

  • 11

    Did you visit Yulong Mountain?

    你去玉龙雪山了吗？

  • 12

    I just stayed at home to read, play games, and relax, but it was still fun.

    我就待在家里看书、玩游戏、放松，但假期仍然很有趣。

常用搭配

2 句
  • 13

    Yes, we stayed in the old town for a few days, and we enjoyed it very much.

    是的，我们在老城住了几天，玩得很开心。

  • 14

    It is! It just took my breath away.

    是的！它让我惊叹不已。
